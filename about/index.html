MY STORY

Hi! I'm Lakshmi. I'm an engineer by degree (Medical Electronics), but I spent all 4 years of college in our design team, haha.

At KPMG, I work as an Associate Consultant in Digital Transformation. On the side, I obsess over product design and product management. I kept noticing that the products people loved weren't always the best looking or the most powerful. So I started taking them apart to figure out why, and behavioural economics kept turning up as the root cause.

That question is how I design now: how do I make someone pick this, stay, and pay without feeling guilty about it? This page is where I take products apart, then rebuild them with personas, user flows, and brand strategy to show what I'd do differently.

The design craft comes from college. Across 8 fests I went from design volunteer to junior coordinator to senior coordinator, then mentor in my final year. We made logos, posters, stamps, and merchandise, and footfall went from literally 0 (thanks, covid) to over 40k. In 2023 I was design head of the Association of Medical Electronics, where I built the whole brand, the Instagram grid, and templates that got member write-ups out as posts much faster.

I design products people choose, and I study why they do.
